Public Skating & Adult Skate 18+ - Participants must skate forwards in one direction.
Figure Skating - Maximum of 24 skaters. For experienced skaters to practice jumps, spins and the fundamentals of figure skating.
Parent & Tot Skate - For parents to teach their children, six years old and under how to skate. Max. ratio of one adult 18+ to three children. Skates and CSA approved helmets only, no sticks or pucks.
                           CSA approved helmets are strongly recommended for all on-ice activities. Participants are encouraged to wear additional safety equipment consistent
                                                                      with the sport/activity to which they have chosen to participate.
                         Payment is required at the Customer Service Kiosk BEFORE you may participate in a program. All participants must wear a wristband for entry onto the ice.
                                                              Admission is per person. Check in begins half an hour before program start time.
                                                  Skaters under the age of 10 must be accompanied by a guardian over the age of 16 who remains on-site.
