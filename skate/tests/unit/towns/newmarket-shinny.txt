Shinny: A recreational time that often results in a game of pick-up hockey. Full equipment is mandatory, including CSA approved helmets. Players 17 years and under are required to wear a mouth guard,
neck guard and full face protection. All participants must play within their age group.

Admission: Refer to program details for more information. This schedule is subject to change without notice. Payment is required at the kiosk BEFORE you may participate in a program. All participants must
be present at the Customer Service kiosk at time of payment. Check in begins half an hour before the program start time.
